# Naming Options

## Recommendation

Use:

- Brand: `Atlas`
- Category: `Agent Payments OS`
- Core tagline: `Let agents pay without losing control.`

This is the clearest and strongest structure because `Atlas` feels infrastructural and premium while the category explains the product immediately.

## Best options

### 1. Atlas

- strongest balance of trust, scale, and memorability
- works well with serious enterprise positioning
- pairs cleanly with `Agent Payments OS` or `The control plane for agent spending`

### 2. Axiom

- more technical and precise
- feels premium and abstract in a good way
- strongest alternative if you want a less literal, more elite brand

### 3. Meridian

- polished and directional
- works well for a product framed as a control plane
- slightly softer than Atlas, but still premium

### 4. Sentinel

- strongest on trust and oversight
- good fit if operator control becomes the dominant story
- slightly more security-coded than Atlas

### 5. Keystone

- strong systems and infrastructure signal
- good fit for a platform that anchors many services
- less elegant than Atlas or Axiom, but credible

### 6. Relay

- modern and product-friendly
- suggests orchestration and controlled movement
- slightly lighter and less commanding

### 7. Foundry

- feels like infrastructure and serious tooling
- useful if the broader story becomes about building agent commerce workflows
- more industrial than premium

### 8. Northstar

- polished and strategic
- good for roadmap and enterprise story
- more aspirational and less operational

## Recommended brand architecture

- `Atlas`
- `Agent Payments OS`
- `The control plane for agent spending`

## Alternate taglines

- `Controlled autonomy for agent commerce.`
- `Approve, pay, and audit agent actions.`
- `Safe spend for real-world AI agents.`
- `The trust layer for agent payments.`

## Decision note

If you keep the current name, do not rename the product surface to something more complicated. `Atlas` is already the best default. Only switch if you want a more abstract premium brand, in which case `Axiom` is the best fallback.
